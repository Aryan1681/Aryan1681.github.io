01side quests

What I do when I'm not coding.

Every one of these ends up shaping how I write software, whether I mean it to or not.

quest_01 · main quest outside the IDE

Tennis

Tennis is where I train consistency and patience. You don't win a long rally with one great shot. You win it by getting the next ball back, again and again.

// lesson: Same as debugging: steady beats flashy.

quest_02 · in moderation

Valorant

I play for the strategy and the teamwork, not the drama or the ego. Clear comms, trusting your teammates and adapting mid-round are just good engineering habits with a timer on them.

// lesson: Comms > aim. Same in standups.

quest_03 · always on

Going under the hood

I like learning new languages and finding out what's below the abstraction: C++ and Assembly for fun, Linux internals at work, whatever the next layer down is.

// lesson: Abstractions are great until they leak.

quest_04 · 1,000+ visitors a day

Helping people, at scale

Working the front desk at ASU's libraries taught me more about UX than any course. Every confused visitor is a bug report for the system around them.

// lesson: Users don't read docs. Design for that.
